# ASTR 102: Introduction to Stellar and Solar Systems

## 📖 Course Overview & Syllabus
This course covers basic concepts of astronomy related to our Solar System. Topics include units of cosmic distances, astronomy in Islamic civilization, the electromagnetic spectrum and telescopes, characteristics of the solar system and Kepler's laws, the Moon-Earth-Sun system and related phenomena, terrestrial planets, Jovian planets, and the origins of asteroids and comets.

## 📝 Basic Info
* **Academic Level:** Level 03 (Second Year)
* **Prerequisites:** MATH 101
* **Core Focus:** Astronomy and Planetary Science
